# Data Sources

## SEBI Investor Survey 2025

Official source (SEBI Research reports and statistics):

https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/jan-2026/investor-survey-2025-_99170.html

This is a SEBI study of investor behaviour. It is **not** an INDmoney app-usage or conversion dataset — do not conflate the two when interpreting results.

Place the downloaded workbook(s) in `data/raw/` once obtained. Do not assume filenames, sheet names, or column definitions ahead of inspecting the actual file(s) — the first analysis notebook (`analysis/01_data_inspection.ipynb`) is where that inspection happens.
