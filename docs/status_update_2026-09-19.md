# Dashboard Update — 2026-09-19

## What we've done

- **Sourced and cleaned the data.** SEBI Investor Survey 2025 (109,430 respondents), narrowed to our focused group: 553 salaried respondents, aged 18–28, who've considered mutual funds but don't currently hold one.
- **Built the full analysis dashboard** — dataset & method, a research-measure register, and a 9-theme analysis view (motivations & goals, barriers, previous investment & stopping reasons, risk preferences, reported uncertainty, group differences by income/experience, awareness & media, encouragement factors, learning preferences).
- **Completed the descriptive analysis** across all 9 themes, with every number traceable back to the raw survey data.
- **Turned the analysis into 10 concrete "reported problems"** — each backed by specific respondent counts and percentages (e.g. fear of losing money, not knowing how to start, information overload, distrust of fund managers, long investment horizon concerns, disappointing returns, cost/minimum-amount barriers, competing financial needs, unclear financial concepts).
- **Grouped those 10 problems into 5 themes** (affinity mapping): risk & returns, understanding & getting started, information overload, trust, and fitting investing around other financial needs.
- **Drafted an initial proposition**: a guided investing experience — simple explanations, clear comparisons, transparent fund information, an understandable payment commitment — to help a user assess risk, resolve questions, and complete SIP setup, while still letting them wait or reconsider.

**Bottom line:** we know what the *reported* problems are and roughly what we think needs solving.

## What's pending

- **Everything above is SEBI survey data — a national population, not INDmoney's users.** We don't yet know if these reported problems actually show up in INDmoney's product.
- **Next step: validate against INDmoney itself.**
  - Do a first-time-user onboarding walkthrough and start an SIP ourselves, end to end.
  - Do a structured product/screen-by-screen breakdown of that flow.
  - Check which of the 5 problem themes actually appear concretely in INDmoney's interface — not just "respondents in general report this," but "this specific screen/step in INDmoney's app has this problem."
- **Only after that validation**, refine the proposed solution so it targets what's actually broken in INDmoney, not just what the survey suggests in general.

## Hurdles / challenges

- **The core gap:** the SEBI survey can tell us what a broader population *reports* as barriers — it cannot, by itself, prove any of that is true of INDmoney specifically. Closing that gap requires hands-on product testing, which hasn't started yet.
- This means our current 5 themes and proposition are **hypotheses to test**, not confirmed findings about INDmoney — the upcoming onboarding/SIP walkthrough is where we'll find out which of them hold up, which don't, and whether the proposed direction needs to change.
- This is the next concrete phase of work, immediately following the analysis phase we just completed.
